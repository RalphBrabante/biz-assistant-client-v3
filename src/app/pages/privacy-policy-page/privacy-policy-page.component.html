Biz Assistant ("we", "our", "us") processes personal and business data to provide inventory, orders, invoices, expenses, reporting, and organization management features in our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, role, organization memberships.
  • Business records: items, customers, vendors, orders, invoices, expenses, and reports.
  • Uploaded files: receipts, profile images, and supporting documents.
  • System data: access logs, audit/activity records, permissions, and security events.

2. How We Use Data

  • Operate and secure your organization workspace.
  • Authorize user access based on roles and permissions.
  • Generate reports, notifications, and operational metrics.
  • Send service emails such as password setup/reset and key workflow alerts.

3. Storage and Third-Party Services

  • Data is stored in application databases and related infrastructure services.
  • File uploads may be stored in local storage, DigitalOcean Spaces, or Google Drive based on admin settings.
  • Email delivery may be processed by SMTP2GO for transactional notifications.

4. Access Control and Retention

  • Users can only access data allowed by assigned role, permission set, and organization scope.
  • We retain data needed for operational, accounting, audit, and compliance requirements.
  • Revoked or expired organization licenses may restrict access to platform data.

5. Your Responsibilities

  • Protect account credentials and use strong passwords.
  • Ensure uploaded files and entered records comply with applicable laws and policies.
  • Promptly report suspected unauthorized access to your administrator.

6. Contact

For privacy-related concerns, contact your Biz Assistant system administrator or your designated support channel.
